The Starter Kit is developed by Wolff Solutions. The organization deploying a derived app is responsible for that app’s data practices. Before publishing a modified app, replace this demo notice with the operator’s identity, privacy contact, actual providers, collection and retention practices. Adding authentication, analytics, forms or AI changes the practices this notice describes.

Hosting and technical requests

Requests to the published site pass through Cloudflare. Hosting, security and operational systems may process IP addresses, browser and device information, requested URLs, timestamps and error details to deliver and protect the service. Avoid putting sensitive information in URLs or diagnostic reports.

Retention and your choices

You can clear this site’s browser data in your browser settings. This does not delete records already sent to a server or another provider. Contact us to request access, correction or deletion of information we control, or to ask about retention. We may need to verify your request and retain information needed for security or legal obligations. Do not send passwords, API keys or access tokens by email.
