# Bundled skill sources

The `web-perf` skill is copied from https://github.com/cloudflare/skills/tree/main/skills/web-perf and retains the upstream Apache-2.0 license in its directory.

`wf-web-lighthouse` follows the reusable Wolff skill at https://github.com/MrBrunoWolff/the-skills/tree/main/skills/wf-web-lighthouse. The other starter skills are focused, self-contained guidance maintained with this kit. `modern-web-guidance` retrieves current guidance from GoogleChrome/modern-web-guidance rather than vendoring its data.

The navigation and shared visual tokens follow the authorized the-system-one reference. Dependencies retain their upstream licenses in their installed packages.
